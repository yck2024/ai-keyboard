How this is calculated

  • Gemini audio is counted at 32 tokens per second (Gemini token docs). Groq Whisper is billed per hour of audio.
  • Transcript tokens come from words per minute, at roughly 100 tokens per 60–80 English words. Japanese and Chinese tokenise differently.
  • The Gemini Live line assumes the transcript is billed as text output, which is the cautious choice.
  • Writing styles send the transcript plus your prompt; the estimator ignores the prompt overhead and any minimum billing per request.
  • Your real numbers are in Settings → Cloud & privacy, which lists tokens used request by request.
