A reasonable start: use Groq for fast cloud dictation and rewriting, or Gemini for cloud voice and writing; add the offline pack for when you have no signal. ChatGPT is another text-cleanup choice: sign in with your own ChatGPT plan, no API key, text only. Cleanup takes a few seconds because of how OpenAI responds, not because of the app; choose Groq or Gemini when speed matters. Free-plan limits and prices change, so check provider pages.

How your key is used

  • You paste it into Settings → Cloud & privacy (or the setup wizard). It is stored encrypted on your device.
  • Requests go from your phone straight to the provider. AI Keyboard has no server in between.
  • Groq and Gemini require their own API keys. ChatGPT uses your signed-in ChatGPT plan instead; it is for text cleanup only, not dictation or audio.
  • Clearing a key asks for confirmation first.
  • A usage screen in Cloud & privacy shows tokens in and out, request by request, so you can see what you are using.

What will it cost?

For everyday use, very little. The cost estimator turns your minutes of speech per day into a rough monthly figure for Groq and Gemini. The Android speech service, the offline pack and the on-device model are free.
