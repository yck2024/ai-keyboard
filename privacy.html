Last updated: 23 October 2026. The app has no server or account system of its own. Optional ChatGPT sign-in connects your own OpenAI account only on your phone.

YCK built the AI Keyboard app as a free app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains what the app does with your information. If you choose to use the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this policy.

In short. Cloud dictation and cloud writing styles go directly from your phone to the provider you chose, using your own API key for Gemini or Groq, or your own ChatGPT account and plan for ChatGPT text cleanup. Offline SenseVoice dictation, the on-device writing model, and Japanese and Chinese typing stay on your phone. Android speech uses your phone's own speech service and may use the network. I do not run a server that receives your audio, text, keys or ChatGPT sign-in credentials.

Information collection and use

AI Keyboard is designed to collect as little as possible. I do not operate any server that receives your data, and the app has no account system of its own or advertising. Optional ChatGPT sign-in connects your own OpenAI account on your phone, not an account with me. It contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

1. Microphone and voice data

Dictation needs access to your microphone. The app records audio only while the microphone is on, after you tap it, and never in the background. Nothing records until you tap, including in Voice home.

  • Offline SenseVoice: audio is processed on your phone and does not leave it.
  • Android speech: audio is handled by the speech service installed on your phone, which may use the network. It is not guaranteed to be offline.
  • Groq Whisper and Gemini Live: audio is sent to Groq or Google, using your own API key, to be transcribed.
  • No audio and no transcript is saved to disk by the app. If a Groq audio chunk fails to send, it is kept in memory for the current dictation only, and cleared when you finish, cancel or the provider is released.

2. Text and AI processing

To rewrite text with a writing style, the text you choose is sent to the provider you selected: Google Gemini or Groq, using your own API key, or OpenAI, using your own ChatGPT account and plan, or processed on your phone by the optional on-device model. ChatGPT cleanup includes Process Text and optional voice cleanup. Dictation still uses your separately selected speech provider; ChatGPT receives only text for cleanup, not audio. Text is sent only when you trigger an action, for example by stopping a dictation with Stop + AI, holding a style chip, or using the text-selection or share menu. Field Correction reads the focused text box only when you trigger it, and refuses in password, email, address, phone, number and date fields before anything is sent.

Everything you type is processed on your phone for suggestions, auto-fix, prediction and Japanese and Chinese conversion. Keystrokes are not sent anywhere.

3. API keys and settings

Your Groq and Gemini keys are stored encrypted on your device. Your settings, writing styles, learned words and token-usage statistics are stored locally on your device. The app does not back up its data to the cloud. You can clear a key, delete the offline pack and review or delete learned words in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Your ChatGPT sign-in (OAuth) credentials are stored encrypted on your device, never sent to any server of mine or synced. Disconnect clears local access, refresh and ID tokens and attempts revocation at OpenAI; remote revocation may not be confirmed. Encrypted registration details remain for later sign-in. Manage usage opens ChatGPT's usage settings, where you can review app access and limits.

4. Downloads

The offline voice model (SenseVoice, about 228 MiB) and the on-device writing models (Qwen3) are downloaded only when you ask for them, from Hugging Face to the app's private storage, and verified by checksum. Like any download, this lets Hugging Face see your IP address.

Permissions

  • Microphone: for dictation, as described above.
  • Internet: to reach the provider you chose, and for model downloads.
  • Vibrate: for key haptics.

Third-party services

When you choose them, these services receive the data described above under their own terms and privacy policies. I do not control them.

  • Google: Gemini API (Gemini Live and Gemini writing styles) and, if you pick it, your phone's Android speech service. See also the Gemini API terms.
  • Groq: Whisper dictation and Groq writing styles.
  • OpenAI: optional ChatGPT text cleanup using your own ChatGPT account and plan. OpenAI's terms, retention practices and plan limits apply. Requests use store:false, but this does not guarantee zero retention.
  • Hugging Face: one-time model downloads.
  • Google Play: distribution of the app. If you installed from Google Play, Google may share aggregated diagnostics, such as crash reports, with the developer through Play Console, under Google's policies and your device settings.

Log data

The app has no crash reporting or analytics of its own and does not send log data to me. If you turn on debug logging in Help & About → Troubleshooting, logs stay on your phone until you copy or clear them yourself.

This website

This policy covers the app. The website you are reading is separate: it uses Google Analytics to count visits, so I can see roughly how many people use these pages and which pages they read. Google Analytics sets cookies or similar identifiers in your browser and receives your IP address, browser and device details, and the pages you view, under Google's privacy policy. The site does not send anything you type into it, such as the numbers in the cost estimator, and it never handles API keys, audio or app data. You can opt out with your browser's tracking protection or Google's browser add-on. The app's own statement above, that it contains no analytics SDK, is unchanged.

Security

I value your trust and use commercially reasonable means to protect your information, such as encrypted key storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

This site and the app may link to other sites. If you click a third-party link, you are sent to that site, which I do not operate. I strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit. I have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, policies or practices of third-party sites or services.

Children's privacy

These services do not address anyone under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not send data to me, I hold none, but if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact me so that I can take the necessary action.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. I will post the new policy on this page, with the date above, so please review it periodically. Changes are effective when posted.

Contact

If you have questions or suggestions about this policy, contact support@kengblog.com, or use the feedback form.
